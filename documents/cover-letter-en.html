Cover Letter — Concordia

Academic exchange · Semester S9 · Montréal

Roch Berquieres · Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT · roch.berquieres@etu.toulouse-inp.fr

To the International Exchange Selection Committee
Concordia University — Montréal, Canada

Subject: Application for an exchange semester in Electrical Engineering

Dear Members of the Selection Committee,

I am an electrical engineering student at Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT in France, where I follow a work-study programme alongside my apprenticeship at Ascent Integration. I would like to spend my S9 semester at Concordia University to strengthen my knowledge of automatic control and gain experience studying in an international environment.

My apprenticeship has helped me understand what working as an automation engineer involves. I develop Siemens PLC programmes and HMI screens, contribute to testing and troubleshooting, and have taken part in commissioning work in Germany. I enjoy seeing how ideas studied in class are applied to real equipment, but I also want to go further in the theory behind these systems. Studying control engineering at Concordia would help me build that stronger foundation.

This exchange would complement my professional experience in a way that my current apprenticeship cannot. It would give me the opportunity to study different approaches to engineering, work with students from other backgrounds and become more confident using English in technical situations. Living in Montréal would also be a personal challenge: adapting to a new university, building a routine abroad and discovering another culture.

Before joining Ascent Integration, I completed an apprenticeship at ALTEO R&D, where I worked with laboratory measurements and experimental methods. Both experiences have taught me to be thorough, curious and comfortable learning on the job. I would bring that same attitude to my studies at Concordia.

My longer-term goal is to become an automation project manager in the aerospace industry. I see this exchange not as a break from my professional path, but as a way to broaden it — technically, professionally and personally.

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,
Roch Berquieres
