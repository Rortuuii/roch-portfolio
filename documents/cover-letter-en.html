Cover Letter — Concordia

Academic exchange · Semester S9 · Montréal

Roch Berquieres · Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT · roch.berquieres@etu.toulouse-inp.fr

To the Incoming Exchange / Admissions Team
Gina Cody School of Engineering and Computer Science
Concordia University, Montréal, Québec, Canada

Subject: Academic exchange application — Electrical Engineering / Control Systems — Semester S9

Dear Admissions Committee,

I am currently an Electrical Engineering student at Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT in France, completing a work-study engineering programme at Ascent Integration. I would like to express my strong interest in spending my S9 exchange semester at Concordia University, subject to nomination and the exchange agreements between our institutions.

My main academic goal is to deepen my understanding of automatic control and industrial automation. I am particularly interested in studying feedback control, system modelling and control of electrical equipment. Concordia's Electrical Engineering curriculum, including subjects such as Fundamentals of Control Systems (ELEC 372), appears closely aligned with this objective. Any course selection would remain subject to prerequisites, availability and formal academic approval.

My experience combines experimental engineering and industrial automation. During my previous apprenticeship at ALTEO R&D, I carried out measurements and contributed to a design-of-experiments approach for alumina powders. At Ascent Integration, I develop Siemens PLC programs and HMI interfaces, participate in testing and troubleshooting, and have taken part in on-site commissioning in Germany. These experiences have strengthened both my technical curiosity and my interest in reliable, practical control systems.

Beyond the technical aspects, studying at an English-speaking university in Montréal would help me become more independent, communicate more confidently in an international environment and discover another approach to engineering education. I also serve as treasurer and a climbing instructor in my university climbing club, which has developed my sense of responsibility and teamwork.

In the longer term, I hope to become an automation project manager in the aerospace industry. I believe that an exchange at Concordia would provide an important academic and personal step towards this ambition.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be pleased to provide further information about my background and study plan.

Yours faithfully,
Roch Berquieres
