Statement of Purpose

Concordia University · Control & Automation

Roch Berquieres · Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT · roch.berquieres@etu.toulouse-inp.fr

My plan at Concordia

For my S9 semester, I would like to study at Concordia University in Montréal and focus on graduate-level courses in automatic control and electrical machines. I want to understand the theory behind the control systems I use at work.

The courses I want to study

ELEC 6061 : Real-time Computer Control Systems. I am interested in digital and real-time control, sampling, stability and practical implementation constraints. This course would help me connect what I learn about feedback control at ENSEEIHT with the software and equipment used in industrial automation.

ELEC 6091 : Discrete Event Systems. Automata, Petri nets and supervisory control are closely related to my work with PLC sequences, operating modes and industrial machine logic. I want to develop a more systematic approach to modelling these systems.

ELEC 6491 : Controlled Electric Drives. I want to explore motor control, power converters, induction machines and field-oriented control in greater depth. This would build on both my experience with Siemens drives and my electrical machines and power electronics coursework.

ELEC 6431 : Advanced Electrical Machines and Drives. Advanced modelling of synchronous and induction machines, Park transforms, transient behaviour and vector control are subjects I particularly want to strengthen. Understanding these models would help me analyse and control electric machines more confidently.

How this builds on my apprenticeship

At Ascent Integration, I develop Siemens PLC programmes and HMI interfaces, and I help test and commission industrial equipment for the aerospace sector. My apprenticeship has given me practical problem-solving experience. These courses would give me a deeper understanding of control laws, dynamic models and electric drives, helping me link engineering theory with what happens on site.

What I want to gain personally and professionally

Studying in Montréal would help me use English every day and meet engineering students from other countries. Living abroad would also help me become more independent. In the longer term, I want to develop from an automation engineering role into managing automation projects in the aerospace industry.
