Statement of Purpose

Concordia University · Control & Automation

Roch Berquieres · Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT · roch.berquieres@etu.toulouse-inp.fr

Primary choice — Concordia University

My priority for my third-year S9 international exchange is Concordia University, Montréal, because I want to study control engineering in an English-speaking academic environment. ÉTS Montréal and Polytechnique Montréal remain alternatives while eligibility and course access are assessed.

Academic priority — Control & Automation

I want to consolidate automatic control fundamentals: modelling dynamic systems, feedback, stability, controller design and industrial applications. ELEC 372 (Fundamentals of Control Systems) is an example of my course interests, not a confirmed registration. Electrical machines and power electronics are complementary, not the main focus.

Professional background

At Ascent Integration I develop Siemens PLC applications and HMI systems and contribute to industrial verification and commissioning. Previously, ALTEO R&D strengthened my instrumentation and experimental methods. I now want to connect practical automation with a deeper theoretical understanding of control.

Career connection

My development path is technical expertise → greater autonomy on industrial projects → automation project management in the aerospace sector. An international semester would support this path through specialist coursework and intercultural teamwork.

Validation required

Host-university admission, exchange eligibility, courses offered during S9, prerequisites, credit equivalences and the Learning Agreement must all be confirmed with Concordia and Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT.
